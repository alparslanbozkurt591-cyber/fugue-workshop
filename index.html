Exposition

Each voice enters in turn: subject in the tonic, answer in the dominant (tonal adjustments to the head), with the previous voice carrying the countersubject.

Episodes

Sequences built from the subject's opening motif, often imitated in a second voice, modulating to the next key.

Middle entries

The subject returns in related keys, such as the relative major or minor and the subdominant, with the countersubject in invertible counterpoint.

Stretto and close

Overlapping entries at the tightest offset that stays consonant, a final entry in the tonic, then a IV–V–I cadence.
